This dashboard shows how much crude oil India buys from Russia between 2021 and 2026. Before 2022, Russia was a very small supplier, but today it is one of India’s biggest. The cards at the top give the big picture: the peak import was 2.70 million barrels a day, the lowest was 1.05, the average is 1.83, and Russia’s share reached 35% of India’s oil. The line chart tells the story over time. After new sanctions in November 2025, imports fell to 1.05 by February 2026, but then they bounced back fast and hit a record 2.70 in June 2026. [Move the month slider] If I zoom into this period, you can clearly see the fall and the recovery. The column chart shows how much India paid: only 1.1 billion dollars in 2021-22, which jumped to 54 billion in 2023-24, and then eased to 50.2 billion. [Click a year tile] When I click one year, the whole dashboard changes to show only that year, and I can click again to reset. The bar chart shows Russia’s share growing from 2% to 35% in just three years. In short, Russia went from a small supplier to a major one, sanctions caused a sharp fall, and imports recovered quickly. Thank you!
